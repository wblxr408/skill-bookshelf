# Narrative Hero

Use this style for emotion-first visuals that should make a system feel vivid, premium, and memorable.

## Core Intent

Make the viewer feel the project before explaining every detail:
- atmosphere
- stakes
- personality
- relationship between the human and the system
- emotional signature of the product

This is not a dashboard mockup and not a process chart. It should feel like a flagship hero illustration or campaign poster.

## Hallmarks

- cinematic wide composition, usually `16:9`
- clear title-safe area, often on the left
- one rich story scene on the right or center
- warm/cool lighting contrast
- environment props that imply the system's capabilities
- floating holograms, cards, symbols, or mini-scenes
- original small mascots or symbolic helpers
- emotionally coherent visual identity
- premium landing-page hero energy

## Use For

- website hero banners
- flagship brand posters
- product launch visuals
- emotional research/product storytelling
- narrative AI system concepts

## Scene Logic

Instead of explicit arrows and boxes, show system capabilities through:
- props on a desk, wall, lab, studio, terminal, notebook, shelf
- holographic panels, branching light structures, glowing artifacts
- small characters performing roles
- lighting and posture that reveal the user's state
- environment details that encode the domain

## Prompt Vocabulary

Useful phrases:
- cinematic hero illustration
- flagship product visual
- emotionally engaging research scene
- cozy night studio
- warm tungsten light and cool cyan holograms
- premium landing-page hero image
- story-driven product branding
- human-in-the-loop AI copilot

## Text Handling

- Keep in-image text minimal.
- Use title, subtitle, maybe a few short labels on props or holograms.
- Let imagery carry meaning.

## Negative Tendencies

Avoid:
- generic dashboard screenshot
- empty sci-fi room
- messy prop overload
- childish cartoon energy
- purple cyberpunk default
- copied anime character tropes
- literal flowchart disguised as a scene

## Prompt Skeleton

Use or adapt this structure:

```text
Create an original cinematic hero illustration for <system/project name>.

Scene and composition:
16:9 landscape, dark title-safe area on the left, main story scene on the right, premium landing-page hero image.

Main subject:
<who the human is, what they are doing, what emotional state they are in>

System visualization:
Represent the system through holograms, props, miniature original mascots, floating cards, branching light structures, and subtle symbolic interfaces.

Environment details:
<desk/studio/lab/library/city/night/etc>

Lighting and mood:
warm amber practical light, cool cyan interface glow, emotionally intelligent contrast.

Design goals:
make the system feel alive, rigorous, memorable, and human-centered; not a dashboard, not a flowchart, not copied from any reference.
```
