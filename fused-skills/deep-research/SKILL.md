# Deep Research

Read [the execution contract](references/fusion-policy.md). Preserve topic research, quick brief, literature review, fact-check, WHY/HOW/WHAT three-way scan, Socratic research mentoring, systematic review/meta-analysis, and optional literature monitoring. Load only the requested mode and source/methodology/ethics/reporting references from [the research module](modules/research/PLAYBOOK.md).

Manuscript paper-review is unified under `$paper-audit`; read its evidence-review route. New research still uses this skill's evidence hierarchy, search/identifier verification, question/method design, synthesis, bias assessment, statistical/reporting guidance, preregistration, ethics, and research-to-writing handoff. Full research-to-publication remains `$academic-pipeline`. Optional future monitoring requires the user's scheduling request.

Use specialist perspectives when warranted and authorized; do not force thirteen agents for a bounded lookup. Separate source evidence, inference, and uncertainty. [Runtime adapters](references/runtime-adapters.md) cover source-suite external dependencies and current tools. Existing scripts/templates/role definitions remain accessible.
