# evidence review

Use the manuscript's actual claims as the search/verification scope. Retain deep-research paper-review capabilities: research question and novelty, source hierarchy and exact citation verification, methodology adequacy, cross-source synthesis, argumentation/fallacies, statistical/reporting quality, risk of bias, ethical/IRB reasoning, reproducibility, reporting guidelines, and unresolved conflicts. Read only the relevant evidence-module source/methodology/argumentation/ethics/reporting references.

Search current primary literature only when necessary; preserve identifiers and distinguish author claims from verified findings. Systematic-review manuscripts may need PRISMA, preregistration, bias/meta-analysis/reporting guidance from that module. Do not run a new thirteen-role topic research pipeline for a narrow manuscript review. For a new research project call deep-research. A citation match is not proof that the source supports the claimed conclusion.
