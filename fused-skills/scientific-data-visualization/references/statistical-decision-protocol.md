# Statistical decision protocol

Use formulas only for a confirmed method that needs explanation, derivation, or custom implementation. Method recommendation needs a compact decision packet, not mathematical notation or raw rows.

For group comparison, require: named numeric outcome, named grouping column, declared independent/paired/repeated design, experimental unit, independent-observation review, missing-data review, outlier/recording-error review, outcome type, and at least two non-empty groups. For continuous independent-group inference, also require a predeclared minimum meaningful standardized effect size and target power; the gate calculates achieved power from the available group sizes. A group with fewer than three valid observations is blocked. A Shapiro result is reported as sample-limited below eight observations and can never by itself establish normality.

The local gate runs Shapiro diagnostics per group and Levene's variance diagnostic. It reports `eligible`, `needs-confirmation`, or `blocked`; only `eligible` may become a recommendation. For two groups, the eligible parametric variant is Welch t rather than choosing pooled variance after a variance pre-test. Failure of a parametric diagnostic does not silently select a rank test: ordinal/rank analyses additionally require a scale, ties, and distribution-shape review; a mean-comparison question with failed normality is held for analyst review.

Do not infer independence, pairing, repeated measurement, causal intent, experimental unit, missing-at-random status, or an adequate sample size from column names. A power computation assesses precision for the declared effect target; it does not repair biased sampling, pseudoreplication, or a poor measurement instrument. Strict adapters currently cover independent continuous/ordinal groups, paired continuous/ordinal pairs, repeated ordinal and reviewed continuous conditions, independent binary groups, factorial two-/three-way ANOVA, Pearson and reviewed partial association, Kaplan--Meier eligibility, Cox qualification with external power evidence, selected power calculations, linear/binary/ordinal regression qualification, PLS qualification, discriminant-analysis qualification, and exploratory clustering qualification. These adapters grant only a method recommendation or controlled-development eligibility; model-performance claims remain blocked until training, tuning, calibration, and independent validation artifacts exist.

The AI receives only: the user goal, compact profile, diagnostics, status, and eligible method IDs. It must not receive formulas unless the explicit double confirmation is satisfied.
